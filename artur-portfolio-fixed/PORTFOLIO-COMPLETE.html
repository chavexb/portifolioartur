Selected project / Animation Supervisor

Masters of the Universe: Legends Unite

Animation SupervisorMagic Media1 video

As Animation Supervisor on Masters of the Universe: Legends Unite, I took a very hands-on role in the project. I reviewed shots closely, gave detailed feedback, and made sure the team stayed on track. I also handled direct communication with the client, which meant I was responsible for understanding their needs and making sure our work met their expectations. The project itself was enjoyable to work on, and the team did an excellent job keeping everything on schedule. I want to thank my teammates for their hard work and dedication—they made this project run smoothly. I also want to thank Magic Media for giving us the chance to work with such a well-known and beloved IP.
